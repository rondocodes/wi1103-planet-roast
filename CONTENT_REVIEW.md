## Blocking issues

### Q5: the exact IPAT result is absent

1.35 × 4 = 5.4. Keeping impact constant requires T_new/T_old = 1/5.4, approximately 0.1852: an 81.48% reduction in intensity. None of A–E is exactly 5.4. Five is insufficient. Six would be a sufficient integer reduction only if the question explicitly required a whole-number factor that meets or exceeds the target. It does not.

### Q6: the Earths denominator is missing

Earths required uses global biocapacity per person, not Indonesia's biocapacity per person. The calculation 1.8/1.2 = 1.5 gives a national-capacity ratio. Do not silently turn that into 1.5 Earths. A consistent-year global denominator is necessary; do not substitute a number from a different account edition.

### Q7: the comparison does not isolate technology

A and B can both illustrate why population alone is insufficient. Neither controls for affluence, energy supply, trade, and other causes. B may be a course intention, while A more directly compares similar populations; do not force either without the course key. The attribution/classification involving Commoner also warrants lecture checking.

### Q33: undefined RE and conflicting labels

For the conventional fractional rebound definition, R = 1 − actual savings / engineering-expected savings. No rebound is R = 0; full rebound is R = 1; backfire is R > 1, with total use above the baseline. Original option B has the compatible inequality, but the prose and other labels are inconsistent and RE is not defined. Keep it ungraded until the course definition/key is available.
